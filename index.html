Signal Fundamentals

How Binary Signals Work

Digital systems communicate using two fundamental states: 0 (LOW) and 1 (HIGH). Logic gates process these binary signals to produce meaningful outputs.

Toggle Bitstream Data:
Electronics Principle: In TTL (Transistor-Transistor Logic) digital circuits, a binary 0 (LOW) corresponds to a voltage potential close to ground (0V to 0.8V), whereas a binary 1 (HIGH) corresponds to positive supply voltage (2.4V to 5.0V). Logic gates contain transistor networks that switch between these voltage thresholds in fractions of a nanosecond.
